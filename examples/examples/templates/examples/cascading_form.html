Cascading dropdowns

Country → region → city, queried and validated server-side

Try it: pick a country to load its regions, then a region to load its cities. When a parent changes, Datastar POSTs the form and the server re-renders the fragment with the child options repopulated and any now-invalid child selection reset. A pending indicator shows while options load. The server authoritatively validates that each child belongs to its parent.

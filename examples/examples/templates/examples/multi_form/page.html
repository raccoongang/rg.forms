Multi-form submission

Two forms and a formset submitted together — ADR-0005

Try it: submit with a blank username and a blank birth year — both members' errors appear at once in a single SSE patch (non-short-circuit validation). Then set birth year to 2010 and any work-experience start year to 2015 to trip the cross-form rule (work history before age 16).

One reactive_forms_response() call validates all three members, patches this one shared fragment on any error, and SSE-redirects on all-valid. Persistence and the cross-form check stay in the view — the helper is submission plumbing, not a save engine.

First-class reactive widgets vs. correct Django fallback — ADR-0001

The shipped reference template renders a controlled subset as first-class reactive controls (text-family inputs, select, multi-select, checkbox, textarea, date/time). Any other widget falls back to Django's native rendering — correct and submittable, but without reactive data-* attributes until you override the template for your design system. Inspect the fields below:

  • First-class: Text, Select, Multi-select, Checkbox, Textarea, Date — these carry data-bind and reactive attributes.
  • Fallback: Radio, Checkbox group, File — rendered by Django, no reactive attributes.
